# Revenue Analysis of Retail Store

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales transactions and customer behavior to uncover key revenue drivers, purchasing patterns, and customer engagement trends. The goal is to translate raw transactional data into meaningful business insights that support data-driven decision-making.

The analysis combines **SQL for data querying**, **data cleaning and transformation**, and **Power BI for interactive visualization**, simulating a real-world business analytics workflow.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Identify overall sales performance and revenue trends
* Understand customer purchasing behavior and segmentation
* Analyze the impact of subscriptions, discounts, and shipping methods on sales
* Support strategic decisions related to pricing, marketing, and customer retention

---

## 🧩 Dataset Description

The dataset contains customer-level purchase data with the following attributes:

* Customer demographics (age, gender, age group, location)
* Purchase details (item, category, size, color, season)
* Financial metrics (purchase amount, discounts applied)
* Customer behavior indicators (subscription status, purchase frequency, previous purchases)
* Experience metrics (review ratings, shipping type, payment method)

This structure allows for both **sales performance analysis** and **customer behavior analysis**.

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

* **SQL (MySQL):** Data extraction, aggregation, and business queries
* **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards and KPI visualization
* **Excel / CSV:** Data inspection and preprocessing
* **GitHub:** Version control and project documentation

---

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation

Key data preparation steps included:

* Handling missing and inconsistent values
* Standardizing categorical fields (gender, category, season, payment methods)
* Creating derived fields such as age groups and purchase frequency metrics
* Validating numeric fields like purchase amount and ratings

These steps ensure the dataset is accurate, consistent, and ready for business analysis.

---

## 🧮 SQL Analysis Highlights

Some of the key business questions answered using SQL:

* What is the total and average revenue generated?
* Which product categories contribute the most to sales?
* How does subscription status affect purchase frequency?
* Which customer segments generate higher value?

SQL queries were designed to mirror real business reporting and performance analysis scenarios.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Insights (Power BI)

The Power BI dashboard presents insights through:

* **Executive KPIs:** Total sales, average order value, customer count, and average rating
* **Sales Performance:** Revenue by category, season, and location
* **Customer Segmentation:** Age groups, gender distribution, and purchasing frequency
* **Customer Experience:** Ratings, discounts, and subscription impact

The dashboard enables quick decision-making by consolidating complex data into a single, interactive view.

---

## 🔍 Key Business Takeaways

* Revenue is concentrated in specific product categories and seasons
* Subscription customers demonstrate higher purchase frequency and potential lifetime value
* Discounts positively influence customer satisfaction and engagement
* Customer demographics play a significant role in purchasing behavior

These insights can guide pricing strategies, marketing campaigns, and retention initiatives.
